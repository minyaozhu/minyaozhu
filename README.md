### Radio and SDR on the Mac

- **[rx888-radio](https://github.com/minyaozhu/rx888-radio)** ([site](https://minyaozhu.github.io/rx888-radio/)): the RX-888 MkII
  software-defined radio on Apple-silicon macOS. A browser SDR on ka9q-radio's `radiod` (HF 0–64 MHz and VHF/UHF),
  FT8 / FT4 / WSPR on every HF band at once with world maps, live airband and public-safety transcription with
  on-device Whisper, SSB voice, AM / SW / FM with stereo, APRS, AIS and SSTV.
- **[malahit-airband-monitor](https://github.com/minyaozhu/malahit-airband-monitor)** ([site](https://minyaozhu.github.io/malahit-airband-monitor/)):
  scan the VHF airband and live-transcribe air traffic control with a Malahit DSP2 receiver on macOS.
